PageFly is a drag-and-drop Shopify page builder aimed at merchants who want more control over landing, product, promotional, and home pages without writing code. We installed PageFly on a Shopify development store, built and published a page, checked its responsive behavior, and explored its CRO tools to see how it performs in a real workflow.

Our main takeaway: **PageFly is easy to learn and capable enough for non-technical merchants to build polished campaign and landing pages quickly, but merchants planning a complete homepage redesign should understand how PageFly interacts with their existing Shopify theme before publishing.**

## Our Verdict

PageFly makes the most sense for Shopify merchants who want to create custom landing or promotional pages without depending on a developer for every change. In our test, the editor was straightforward, the template library gave us a useful starting point, and we were able to build a complete page in roughly 30–40 minutes with some planning done beforehand.

The biggest limitation appeared when we published the PageFly page as the store's Home page. Existing theme-controlled content remained alongside the new PageFly content, creating a visually inconsistent result. That does not make PageFly a poor page builder, but it does mean a full homepage redesign may require additional work in Shopify's theme editor to keep everything visually cohesive.

## What PageFly Offers

![PageFly public homepage presenting page building, testing, analytics, and optimization tools](../../assets/images/blog/pagefly/pagefly-public-homepage-overview.png)

PageFly is a visual page builder for Shopify. Instead of building layouts entirely through a Shopify theme's native sections or custom code, merchants can assemble pages using templates, drag-and-drop elements, Shopify elements, and integrations with third-party apps.

It also goes beyond basic page building with CRO and optimization tools including analytics, A/B testing, heatmaps, section performance, AI page checkup, a cart drawer, and an AEO optimizer. Its public marketing homepage also displays merchant, review, and revenue figures; those are PageFly's claims, not results independently verified in our test.

## What We Tested

We tested PageFly hands-on using its Free plan on a Shopify development store on September 1, 2026. Our test covered installation and onboarding, creating a page, working with a pre-built template, editing elements, checking desktop/tablet/mobile views, publishing a Home-type page, and exploring the main CRO tools available from the dashboard.

We did not evaluate long-term site maintenance, live-chat support quality, a full multi-page build, or the complete paid-plan experience. We also did not independently test competing page builders as part of this review.

## Getting Started With PageFly

Installation was straightforward. After opening PageFly for the first time, we were shown a plan-selection screen before entering the main dashboard. We continued with the Free plan.

The onboarding flow then offered several paths: create a new page using a template or AI, import existing PageFly pages, rebuild pages from the current site, or skip directly to the dashboard. For a first-time user, the flow was clear and did not require technical knowledge.

We initially tried PageFly's AI page-generation workflow, but the Free plan's 10 AI credits were not enough for the full-page generation attempt we made. We therefore switched to the pre-built **Juicy – New Year** template.

This is an important practical limitation of the Free plan: it is useful for trying the editor, but merchants expecting to rely heavily on AI generation may reach the credit limit quickly.

## Building a Page: Our Experience

This was the strongest part of our test.

PageFly organizes the page into sections and provides a visual editing canvas alongside an element library. We could work with standard content such as headings, paragraphs, buttons, images, video, icons, and other PageFly elements, as well as Shopify-native elements. Third-party app elements can also be enabled through PageFly's integrations system.

The editor felt easy to use. We did not need a developer to understand the basic workflow, and the template gave us enough structure to move quickly instead of starting from an empty canvas.

We also checked the page using PageFly's built-in desktop, tablet, and mobile previews. That made it convenient to inspect responsive behavior while editing rather than waiting until the page was published.

With the page structure planned beforehand, the complete build took us approximately **30–40 minutes**.

## The Biggest Issue We Found: Homepage Design Continuity

![The Shopify theme header remaining above a visually different PageFly New Year page design](../../assets/images/blog/pagefly/pagefly-homepage-theme-continuity.webp)

Publishing the page revealed the most important limitation from our test.

We configured the PageFly page as the store's Home page. PageFly's interface indicates that its Home page content works alongside the existing Shopify theme, while theme elements such as the header and footer remain controlled through Shopify's theme editor.

In our test store, the existing theme's navigation and hero content remained visible alongside the new PageFly content. The two designs did not match, so the published homepage looked visually disjointed rather than like one cohesive redesign.

For merchants building standalone landing or campaign pages, this may be much less important. But if your goal is to redesign the entire Shopify homepage with PageFly, plan for additional theme-level work so the existing header, hero, and PageFly sections feel like one design system.

We did not verify whether PageFly provides another built-in workflow that completely avoids this behavior, so our finding is limited to the Home-page publishing setup we tested.

## CRO and Optimization Tools

PageFly includes more conversion-focused functionality than we expected from a traditional page builder.

### Analytics

The analytics dashboard was functional in our development store and displayed metrics including pageviews, sessions, visitors, engaged-session rate, orders, traffic sources, countries, visitor type, and devices. A store with meaningful traffic would naturally provide much more useful data than our low-traffic test environment, but the analytics interface itself was working.

### AI Page Checkup

We were able to run PageFly's AI page checkup, which evaluates page/site health and returns a score out of 100.

### A/B Testing

We could access and use A/B testing while our test store was still on the Free plan. However, PageFly's pricing interface listed A/B testing as a Builder-plan feature when we checked it.

Because the observed access and pricing information did not fully match, merchants choosing PageFly specifically for A/B testing should verify the current plan requirement before making a purchasing decision.

### Heatmaps and Section Performance

Heatmap-related views exposed visitor, traffic-source, and device information, while Section Performance was also functioning with data during our test.

### Cart Drawer and AEO Optimizer

PageFly also includes a Cart Drawer designed around cart-stage offers and product recommendations. Its AEO optimizer is aimed at improving how product information can be understood by AI and answer engines; the interface we tested indicated that the AEO feature currently works on product pages.

These tools make PageFly more interesting for merchants who want page building and conversion optimization in the same product rather than assembling several separate apps.

## Pricing and Value

![PageFly pricing screen showing the Free, Builder, Accelerate, and Enterprise plans with their principal limits](../../assets/images/blog/pagefly/pagefly-pricing-plans-september-2026.png)

We verified the following pricing directly inside PageFly on September 1, 2026. Pricing can change, so merchants should re-check it before subscribing.

| Plan | Price | Key limits/features shown during our test |

| **Free** | **$0/month** | 1 published page or section, 10 AI credits, 320+ page/section templates, 200+ integrations, 24/7 live chat |
| **Builder** | **$24/month** | Starts with 5 published slots, 80 AI credits, A/B testing, AI translation, CRO dashboard analytics; additional slots available |

For merchants experimenting with one page, the Free plan is enough to understand how PageFly works. The one-slot and 10-credit limits make it restrictive for broader production use, however. Stores planning multiple PageFly pages will likely need a paid tier.

## What We Liked

- **Easy visual editor:** We found the core editing workflow approachable even without writing code.
- **Fast page creation:** Starting from a template, we built a complete page in around 30–40 minutes with prior planning.
- **Responsive previewing:** Desktop, tablet, and mobile views are available directly in the editor.
- **Large building toolkit:** PageFly combines templates, PageFly elements, Shopify elements, and third-party integrations.
- **Built-in CRO functionality:** Analytics, testing, page checkup, section performance and other optimization tools extend PageFly beyond page design alone.

## What Could Be Better

- **The Free AI allowance is limited:** Our full-page AI-generation attempt exhausted the available allowance, so the Free plan is better treated as an evaluation tier than an AI-heavy production plan.
- **Full homepage redesigns may require theme work:** Our published Home page retained existing theme-controlled content, creating a visual mismatch with the PageFly design.
- **Feature gating was not completely clear:** A/B testing worked on our Free-plan store even though PageFly's pricing interface associated it with Builder. Merchants should verify access to any must-have feature before choosing a plan.

## Who PageFly Is Best For

PageFly is a strong fit for **non-technical Shopify merchants who regularly need custom landing, promotional, campaign, or product-focused pages** and want more design freedom than their theme provides.

It is particularly attractive when the merchant wants page creation and basic CRO tooling in one environment rather than relying on a developer plus several separate optimization apps.

## Who Should Upgrade or Consider an Alternative

PageFly may be less suitable if your primary goal is to replace every aspect of an existing Shopify homepage without touching the theme itself. Based on our test, merchants pursuing a complete homepage redesign should be comfortable reconciling PageFly content with theme-controlled areas.

Merchants comparing Shopify page builders may also want to evaluate alternatives such as GemPages, EComposer, and Shogun. We did not hands-on test those products for this review, so we are not ranking them against PageFly here.

## Final Verdict

PageFly delivered what matters most from a Shopify page builder in our test: **it made creating a custom page fast and approachable without requiring code.** The editor was easy to understand, templates accelerated the process, responsive checks were built into the workflow, and the additional CRO tools give merchants room to optimize after a page goes live.

The main caveat is that PageFly does not operate in isolation from the Shopify theme. Our Home-page test produced a visible mismatch between existing theme content and the new PageFly design, so merchants planning a complete homepage overhaul should account for that integration work.

For standalone landing and promotional pages, however, PageFly is a strong option to consider—especially for merchants who want to build and iterate without waiting on a developer for every page change.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is PageFly free?

Yes. During our September 1, 2026 test, PageFly offered a Free plan with one published page or section, 10 AI credits, access to 320+ templates, and 200+ integrations. Merchants needing multiple published pages or more AI usage will likely need a paid plan.

### Is PageFly easy to use?

In our hands-on test, yes. We found the template-based editor straightforward and built a complete page in approximately 30–40 minutes with some planning completed beforehand.

### Can PageFly build a Shopify homepage?

Yes, but merchants should understand how it interacts with their Shopify theme. In our test, publishing a PageFly Home page left existing theme-controlled content visible alongside the PageFly content, so additional theme work was needed to create a visually cohesive homepage.

### Does PageFly include A/B testing?

PageFly's pricing interface listed A/B testing under its Builder plan when we checked it. However, A/B testing was accessible on our Free-plan test store. Because of that discrepancy, merchants who consider A/B testing essential should verify the current plan requirement directly before subscribing.
