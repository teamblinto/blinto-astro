## Why a checklist

Because launch day is the worst possible time to notice that your screenshots are 1600px wide on a page that renders them at 1024, or that your app name is truncated in search results, or that the demo store you linked has expired.

We have shipped enough listings to have made all three mistakes. The checklist exists so we make them before submission rather than after.

## Before you write anything

- The one job the app does, in a sentence a merchant would say out loud.
- The three search phrases a merchant would type to find that job. Not your category name — the job.
- The competitor a merchant is most likely to see next to you, and the one thing you do that they do not.

## The listing itself

- App name reads correctly truncated. Check it at about 30 characters.
- Tagline names the job and includes the primary phrase without contorting the sentence.
- First screenshot is the interface doing the job, with realistic data. No logos, no gradients, no phone mockups.
- Screenshots read in order as a story: the problem, the app, the result.
- Pricing table states every limit that could surprise somebody. If a plan caps at 500 orders, that number is on the table.
- Support email resolves and somebody actually reads it.
- Demo store link works, and the demo store has data in it.

## After submission

- Changelog entry on day one, so the listing is not stale before it is indexed.
- A plan for the first ten reviews — who you will ask, and when.
- Somebody named as responsible for review replies, with a target of the same week.

## The one nobody does

Read your own listing on a phone, on mobile data, having not looked at it for a week. Almost every problem worth fixing is visible in that thirty seconds.
